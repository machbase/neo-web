# TagAnalyzer Test Scenarios

## Shared requirement

Every scenario must use `MACHROLL` as its data source. Do not substitute a different table.

## 1. Create a chart

Create a new TagAnalyzer chart, select one or more series from `MACHROLL`, and verify that the chart loads.

## 2. Navigate and zoom

Open a `MACHROLL` chart, zoom in and out, and verify that the visible range changes correctly.

## 3. Analyze a selected range

Select a range on a `MACHROLL` chart, verify its summary values, and open the FFT chart.

## 4. Compare series

Select multiple `MACHROLL` series, open Overlap, and verify that the comparison chart appears.

## 5. Save and reopen

Save a TagAnalyzer board containing a `MACHROLL` chart, reopen it, and verify that its series and settings are preserved.
